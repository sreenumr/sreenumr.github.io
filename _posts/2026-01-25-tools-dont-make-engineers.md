Kubernetes doesn’t make you an engineer.
Neither does Terraform, AWS, or Docker.

Tools amplify thinking.
They don’t replace it.

I’ve seen:
- Over-engineered systems with no monitoring
- Perfect pipelines deploying broken logic
- “Best practices” applied blindly and failing badly

Engineering is about:
- Trade-offs
- Constraints
- Failure modes
- Operational reality

If you can’t explain **why** a tool exists in your system,
you probably don’t need it.

Engineering starts before the tool choice.
